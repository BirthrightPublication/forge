OPTIONAL TRUSTED EXECUTABLES

FORGE does not bundle FFmpeg, FFprobe, Deno or Node.

You may place your trusted Windows copies of ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe here,
or together in tools\ffmpeg\bin\, or configure an existing location in the UI.
They should remain side by side for the download adapter.

Deno or Node is detected through PATH for YouTube processing.
Do not put unrelated executables here or rename a Python package as FFmpeg.
