OPUS-MT English to Russian

Publisher: Helsinki-NLP / OPUS-MT, University of Helsinki and contributors.
Registered snapshot:
https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-ru/tree/bb09c99d180016eac6819df3dae68edb1690fdee

The unmodified publisher card identifies the original pretrained model as
opus-2020-02-11.zip:
https://object.pouta.csc.fi/OPUS-MT-models/en-ru/opus-2020-02-11.zip
It describes the transformer-align model, OPUS training data, normalization,
and SentencePiece preprocessing. The registered Hugging Face snapshot uses
the converted Transformers checkpoint format. FORGE downloads and installs
that registered snapshot unchanged; it performs no further weight conversion,
quantization, editing, or fine-tuning. The original model identity and the
publisher's conversion/snapshot declaration remain in the retained documents.

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The Hugging Face checkpoint card separately declares Apache License 2.0:
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
Both scoped declarations and their notices are preserved. Any aggregate
"Apache-2.0 AND CC-BY-4.0" descriptor records these cumulative notice duties;
it does not replace the original CC-BY-4.0 grant or impose additional
restrictions on rights received under that grant. Upstream training-code MIT
terms are a separate scope and are not used as a pretrained-weight license.

Attribution and references requested by OPUS-MT are retained in full in
opus-train-README.md, including:
