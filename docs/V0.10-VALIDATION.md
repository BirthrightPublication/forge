The six full-suite skips cover Windows DPAPI, native maintenance process creation,
two native process-handle cases, managed Windows restart, and the optional pinned
Whisper decoder runtime. Native checks are separate gates; skips are not proof.

Generated tones, colors/images and editable English/Russian text are owned test
fixtures. They demonstrate media/persistence behavior, not neural translation,
voice quality or lip sync. OAuth/upload protocol tests use controlled HTTP and
real generated MP4 bytes; no actual account or external upload is involved.

including archive integrity, private environment execution and unchanged original

[run 42336179391](https://github.com/BirthrightPublication/forge/actions/runs/42336179391)

actual success/failure results.

The active cloud policy denies Hugging Face. A saved settings draft is not an
applied change. Exact new model pins, complete license inventories and actual
Russian translation/reference synthesis therefore remain unresolved, and those
unverified packs stay unavailable. No physical GPU is attached; RTX 5080/CUDA
inference and performance are unverified. Live Google OAuth, verified-channel
access and authorized private upload also remain outstanding. See the
[model inventory](V0.10-MODEL-INVENTORY.md) and
[publication evidence contract](V0.10-RELEASE-GATE.md).

V0.10 remains a development candidate without a tag or stable release. Cloud
tests do not access or modify the owner's `B:\Forge` installation. Final release
requires exact-source native/model/GPU/publishing acceptance, sanitized evidence,
final package/upgrade verification and separate owner approval. Earlier version
approvals and the V0.8 CPU-only exception do not authorize V0.10 publication.
